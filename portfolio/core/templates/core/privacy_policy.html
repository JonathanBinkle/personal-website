Privacy Policy

This site respects your privacy.


Cookies

Two technically-required cookies are used:

  • csrftoken: CSRF protection.
  • sessionid: session handling, set only for site admins.

No tracking cookies.


Logging

Technical logs are kept for troubleshooting (and curiosity). No IP addresses are retained. What's logged per request:

  • Timestamp, request line (method, path, protocol), response status and size
  • Browser user-agent
  • NGINX error logs, IP stripped

Logs are deleted after at most two weeks.
